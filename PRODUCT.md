## Platform
web

## Stack
User-selected Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind and Framer Motion. Bun for dependencies; npm scripts remain supported. Vercel, zero custom configuration.

## Purpose
A light, fun personal profile for Abey Maghari, for family and visitors. Not a professional resume. The only supplied personal fact is her name.

## Constraints
User-approved cream portfolio reference and her supplied photo. Preserve her actual appearance. Normal portrait uses original pixels and a derived alpha matte only. Cartoon is explicitly approved; use GPT Image 2 with the photo as input reference. No invented facts, employment, dates or social links. Editable placeholders live only in src/content/abey.ts and are marked TODO.

## Interaction
Normal/Cartoon toggle. One inventory bag opens an undimmed popover with eight individually toggled accessories. Face remains unobscured; glasses sit above her eyes rather than masking them.

## Approval
Reference-led layout and interactions approved explicitly before implementation.
